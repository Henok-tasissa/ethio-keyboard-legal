# Privacy Policy for Ethio Keyboard

**Effective date:** September 5, 2026

Ethio Keyboard is developed by Henok Tasissa. This Privacy Policy explains how Ethio Keyboard and its custom keyboard extension handle information.

## Information we collect

Ethio Keyboard does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account.

The keyboard's themes, font-size preference, background preference, and key-preview preference are stored locally through the shared app container used by the app and its keyboard extension. The word dictionary and suggestion data are also processed locally by the keyboard extension.

## Keyboard input and Full Access

A third-party keyboard can process text that a user types in an app. Ethio Keyboard uses the text document proxy to insert and delete text, provide suggestions, and support autocorrection behavior.

Ethio Keyboard does not intentionally transmit typed text, passwords, contacts, or other keyboard input to the developer or to a remote server. Ethio Keyboard does not use typed text for advertising or profiling.

Full Access is optional for features such as haptic feedback and any keyboard functionality that requires access beyond the restricted keyboard environment. Users can enable or disable Full Access in iOS Settings. When Full Access is disabled, some features may not work, but the keyboard remains available subject to iOS restrictions.

Users should not grant Full Access to any keyboard unless they understand and trust how that keyboard handles data.

## Third-party services

Ethio Keyboard does not intentionally use advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party tracking SDKs. Apple may process information under Apple's own policies when users download, install, or use the app and App Store services.

## Data retention and deletion

Because Ethio Keyboard does not intentionally send personal information to the developer, there is no developer-held account data to delete. Users can remove local preferences by deleting the app and keyboard extension from their device. Dictionary resources bundled with the app are local resources.

## Children's privacy

Ethio Keyboard does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
