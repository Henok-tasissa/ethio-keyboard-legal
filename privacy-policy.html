Privacy Policy

Ethio Amharic Keyboard

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Ethio Amharic Keyboard (the "app") is developed by Henok Tasissa. This policy explains how the app and its keyboard extension handle information.

The short version

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It works fully offline and does not ask for Full Access.

What the keyboard does with what you type

The keyboard handles the text you type only on your device, to insert letters, show word suggestions, correct spelling, and turn English letters into Amharic. What you type is not stored, logged, or sent anywhere. The keyboard does not request Full Access, so iOS does not allow it to use the network at all.

Settings stored on your device

Your choices (theme, font size, keyboard background, key preview and key click) are saved on your device in a container shared by the app and its keyboard. They never leave your device.

Dictionaries

Word suggestions come from dictionaries that are bundled inside the app. The keyboard does not learn from, or keep a record of, your typing.

Third parties

The app does not include advertising, analytics or any third-party service. Apple may process information about downloads and crashes under Apple's own privacy policy when you use the App Store or TestFlight.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

The developer holds no data about you. To remove the settings stored on your device, delete the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
